Happy 2nd anniversary my love. I honestly can't believe that we have already reached two years together. When I think about everything we have experienced together I realize just how many memories we have created. Two years may sound like a long time but somehow it feels like everything went by so quickly. I still remember the beginning of our relationship and all the little moments that slowly brought us closer together. Since then you have become such an important part of my life and I am so thankful that I get to call you my love.

I want you to know how thankful I am for you. Thank you for all the time you have given me. Thank you for listening to my stories. Thank you for laughing with me. Thank you for being there when I needed someone. There are so many little things you have done that may seem simple but mean so much to me. Every conversation. Every laugh. Every date. Every random picture. Every simple moment. They all became memories that I will always treasure because I experienced them with you.

Of course our relationship has not always been perfect. We have had misunderstandings. We have had difficult moments. We have had days when we didn't completely understand each other. But even through those moments I am thankful that we continued choosing each other. Those moments taught me that love isn't only about the happy days. Love is also about patience. Understanding. Forgiveness. And choosing each other again.

When I think about these two years I realize how many memories we already have. Every conversation. Every laugh. Every date. Every random picture. Every adventure. Every simple day where we were just happy being together. All of those moments became part of our story. They may not seem like huge events to anyone else but they mean something special to me because I experienced them with you. And I want more of those moments.

I don't know exactly what our future will look like. I don't know where life will take us. I don't know what challenges we will face. But I know that I want you to be part of that future. I want to continue growing with you. I want to continue learning about you. I want to continue making you laugh. I want to continue making memories with you. And most importantly I want to continue choosing you.

Thank you for these two beautiful years. Thank you for your love. Thank you for your patience. Thank you for your understanding. Thank you for every memory. Thank you for every laugh. Thank you for every moment that made these two years special. If I could go back to the beginning and experience everything again, I would still choose you.

I hope that this is only the beginning of our story. I hope that we get to celebrate many more anniversaries together. I hope that years from now we can look back at these memories and smile. I hope we can look at the pictures from these years and remember exactly how happy we were. And I hope that no matter how much time passes you will always know how much you mean to me.

You have become one of the most important people in my life. You are someone I can laugh with. Someone I can talk to. Someone I can share my thoughts with. Someone I can make memories with. And someone I want beside me as we continue growing older together.

Two years ago we started our story. Today we are still writing it. And there are still so many pages left for us to fill. More memories. More adventures. More laughs. More pictures. More moments that we will someday look back on and remember.

So on our second anniversary I just want to say thank you. Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for staying. Thank you for loving me. And thank you for giving me two years that I will always treasure. I love you more than I can properly explain with words. Happy 2nd anniversary, my love.
